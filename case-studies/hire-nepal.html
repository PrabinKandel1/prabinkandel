CASE 01 / RECRUITMENT / JOB PLATFORM

Hire-Nepal

A recruitment and job-platform prototype exploring the experience around discovering opportunities, understanding a role and moving toward an application.

PROTOTYPE
PROJECT TYPERecruitment platform

A web prototype focused on the structure and interface of a job-seeking and recruitment experience.

PUBLIC ACCESSPrototype available

The current public prototype can be opened directly from the portfolio.

01 / THE IDEAGive a recruitment workflow a clear interface.

Make the job search easier to follow.

Hire-Nepal explores a service interface for job seekers and employers. The prototype is about the experience: how opportunities are presented, how users move through the product and how the application-oriented path is structured.

It is presented as a prototype, so the portfolio does not attach invented user counts, employers, hires, revenue or production scale to it.

02 / TARGET USERSTwo sides of the recruitment workflow.

Different needs, one product.

JOB SEEKERSDiscover + apply

Find an opportunity, understand the role information and move toward the application flow represented by the prototype.

EMPLOYERSRecruit + manage

Explore the employer-side workflow represented in the product concept without claiming production usage.

04 / INTERFACEProduct UI first, decoration second.

A product built around workflows.

  • Job discovery and browsing
  • Opportunity information
  • Application-oriented interface states
  • Employer workflow concepts
  • Responsive product interface
  • Clear navigation between product areas

These points describe the workflow represented by the prototype rather than claiming a live service behind it.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTIONSoftware and interface implementation.

Shape the experience.

FRONTENDInterface implementation

Translate the product idea into responsive pages and interactive states.

WORKFLOWExperience structure

Keep the journey understandable from discovery through the application-oriented flow.

PRODUCT UIVisual system

Use hierarchy, spacing and interaction to make the service feel coherent.

06 / CHALLENGESWhat the prototype needs to solve clearly.

Keep complexity behind a simple path.

Recruitment products naturally contain different roles, information and actions. The interface challenge is to keep the primary path understandable without pretending that every edge case has already been solved.

The prototype separates the job-seeker experience from employer-oriented actions so each side can have a clearer purpose.

07 / CURRENT STATUSCurrent public state.

A working prototype.

Hire-Nepal is presented as a prototype. No production metrics or business claims are attached to it.

08 / FUTURE DIRECTIONWhat can be developed next.

More depth, without losing clarity.

Further work can deepen the recruitment workflows, refine interface states and continue the prototype toward a more complete product experience. Those are directions, not claims about features already in production.
